Gustavo Madeira Santana is a researcher, computer engineer, neuroscientist, and PhD candidate in the [Emonet Lab](https://emonet.biology.yale.edu) at Yale. He studies how fruit flies use odor information to navigate and is broadly interested in how brains turn sensory input into decisions.

Gustavo maintains this personal site. It contains selected publications, writing, and professional links. It is not a company, laboratory, or commercial service. Use the [publications archive]({{ '/archive/' | relative_url }}) or [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=L603SPwAAAAJ) for research, [GitHub](https://github.com/gumadeiras) for public code, [contact]({{ '/contact/' | relative_url }}) to reach Gustavo, and [privacy]({{ '/privacy/' | relative_url }}) for the site's data practices.
