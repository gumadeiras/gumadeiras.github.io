Gustavo Madeira Santana maintains gumadeiras.com as a static personal site. It has no analytics, ads, tracking pixels, accounts, payments, forms, visitor database, or site cookies. The theme control stores only the value `theme` in your browser's local storage. Its JavaScript does not send that value to the site.

GitHub Pages hosts the site. GitHub and its delivery providers can process standard request data, such as your IP address, URL, time, browser details, and network headers, under their own policies. External sites receive data when you follow their links and apply their own terms. Email is handled by the sender's and recipient's email providers, not by this site.

This page describes the site as inspected on August 22, 2026. Contact [fromweb1@gumadeiras.com](mailto:fromweb1@gumadeiras.com) to report an error.
